Future Impact Initiatives

Pediatric Palliative Care Centre – Coimbatore

Establishment of a dedicated Pediatric Palliative Care Centre to provide holistic medical, emotional, and psychosocial support for children with life-limiting conditions. The center focuses on improving quality of life for children and supporting families through compassionate care services.

Tribal Education & Digital Learning Support Programme

Implementation of digital learning initiatives and educational support systems for tribal children to improve access, retention, and academic performance in remote areas such as Gudalur and surrounding regions.
