Expansion of Para Sports Programme for Neurodiverse Children

Scaling up para sports activities for children with autism and other developmental disabilities to enhance physical fitness, sensory integration, and social inclusion through structured sports training and events.

Advanced Skill Development & Livelihood Programs

Expansion of job-oriented training programs in retail, hospitality, and service sectors with increased focus on placement-linked training for differently abled persons and rural youth.

Tribal Education & Digital Learning Support Programme

Implementation of digital learning initiatives and educational support systems for tribal children to improve access, retention, and academic performance in remote areas such as Gudalur and surrounding regions.

Assistive Devices & Accessibility Support Initiative

Wider distribution of mobility aids, hearing devices, and assistive technologies to improve independence, accessibility, and inclusion for persons with disabilities.

Skill Development & Employment for Blind & Visually Impaired Persons

Swadharma Foundation aims to strengthen livelihood opportunities for blind and visually impaired persons through structured job-oriented training and employment support programs in the IT and manufacturing sectors.
