About Us

Welcome To Swadharma Foundation

Creating Positive Change for Every Life

Swadharma Foundation is a non-governmental social service organization established on July 14, 2022, in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. The Trust was founded by Ms. K. Aruna (Founder) and Mr. K.G. Abishek (Co-Founder), both driven by a deep-rooted commitment to social equity and human dignity.

The name Swadharma is derived from the Sanskrit principle meaning "Purpose of Life" a philosophy that guides every programme and initiative carried out by the Foundation. From its very inception, the organization has attracted like-minded individuals, volunteers, and donors who share the collective vision of uplifting the underprivileged.

The Foundation primarily focuses on two underserved communities: individuals with physical and sensory disabilities, and tribal families living in remote regions. Through targeted interventions ranging from material aid and healthcare support to education and vocational training Swadharma strives to create a more equitable and compassionate society.

Learn about the mission and vision of Swadharma Foundation and its commitment to empowering communities through compassion, inclusion, and sustainable development.

Mission & Vision

Empowering with Compassion

Swadharma Foundation is dedicated to empowering persons with disabilities and tribal communities by improving their socio-economic conditions through inclusive and sustainable development initiatives. The organization focuses on creating equitable opportunities that promote dignity, independence, and long-term well-being.

01

Our Mission

Swadharma Foundation envisions establishing accessible and compassionate Pediatric Palliative Care services in Coimbatore, ensuring that children with life-limiting conditions receive holistic care, dignity, and support.

02

Our Vision

To build a compassionate and inclusive society where every individual has equal opportunities, access to resources, and the freedom to live with dignity, confidence, and purpose. We envision communities that thrive through collaboration, equality, and sustainable progress.

Core Objectives

  • Empower persons with disabilities through structured skill development programs that enhance employability, independence, and dignity.
  • Facilitate inclusive education for visually impaired children through Braille-based and other specialized learning methodologies.
  • Promote educational access and awareness among tribal children, improving enrolment, retention, and learning outcomes in underserved communities.
  • Encourage participation in sports and recreational activities for persons with disabilities, building confidence, resilience, and social inclusion.
  • Develop and strengthen Pediatric Palliative Care services, ensuring holistic care, comfort, and support for children and their families.
  • Foster youth engagement and volunteerism in social development initiatives, creating a culture of empathy and community responsibility.
  • Build sustainable, community-driven models of intervention through collaboration with stakeholders, CSR partners, and local institutions.

A Journey Driven by Passion

Swadtharma Foundation was born from a passion for helping people move and live pain-free. Our founder Mouli Balaraman, driven by this very passion, embarked on a remarkable journey that led to the creation of a trusted physiotherapy practice serving communities across multiple locations.

Year at 1998

Graduation and a Dream in India (1998)

The story begins in India, where our founder graduated with a degree in physiotherapy in 1998. Even during his internship, his vision was clear: to become a private practitioner. He had already secured clinic space and equipment, ready to march on this dream.

Year 1999 – 2004

Building Experience and Expertise (1999 to 2004)

During this period, our founder dedicated himself to gaining valuable clinical experience, working with diverse patient populations and refining his treatment techniques. These formative years laid the foundation for excellence in patient care.

Year at 2005

New Horizons and Opportunities (2005)

A pivotal year that brought new opportunities and challenges. With years of experience under his belt, our founder began exploring innovative treatment approaches and expanding his professional network, setting the stage for future growth.

Year at 2005

Advancing Clinical Practice (2005, continued)

Continued growth and development marked this period, with a focus on specialised treatment protocols and patient-centred care. The commitment to excellence became the hallmark of our practice.

Year 2006 – 2007

Expansion and Growth (2006 to 2007)

These years marked a period of significant expansion. The practice grew, reaching more patients and establishing itself as a trusted name in physiotherapy. New treatment modalities were introduced and the team began to expand.

Year at 2010

Innovation and Excellence (2010)

By 2010, the foundation had become synonymous with quality care and innovative treatment approaches. Investment in advanced equipment and continuing education ensured patients received the best possible outcomes.

Year at 2022

Serving Communities Across Locations (2022)

Today, Swadtharma Foundation stands as a testament to passion, dedication, and unwavering commitment to patient care. With multiple locations serving diverse communities, we continue to help people move better and live pain-free lives.
