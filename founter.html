Our Founder

Aruna Kuppuraj

Founder & CEO

Aruna K is the Founder of Swadharma Foundation. She holds a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Microbiology and has professional experience in the healthcare sector as a Medical Scribe. With a strong foundation in healthcare and a passion for social impact, she is committed to empowering marginalized communities through inclusive education, skill development, and healthcare-focused initiatives. Her vision drives the organization’s efforts in disability empowerment and pediatric palliative care support.

Co-Founder

Abishek

Co-Founder

Abishek Raja is the Co-Founder of Swadharma Foundation. He holds a Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) and worked in the IT sector as a Quality Controller. With a strong technical background and a commitment to social responsibility, he contributes to the Foundation’s mission of driving inclusive development through structured programs in education, skill development, and community empowerment. His analytical expertise and operational experience support the organization in building efficient and impactful initiatives.
